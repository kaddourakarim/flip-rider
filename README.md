# Flip Rider

A simple motorbike flip game for Karim and Léo.

**▶ Play it here: https://kaddourakarim.github.io/flip-rider/**

You can also open `index.html` in a browser. Play with the arrow keys:

- ↑ go · ↓ brake · ← backflip · → frontflip

Finish the race to win a cup. Your score comes from your speed, your flips and the stars you collect.

See [PRD.md](PRD.md) for the full product spec and [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md) for the project log.
